# Ahsora Med Academy — /about Page
## FINAL Developer Specification — v8 (Hybrid, Tightened + Second-Pass Refinements)

**Purpose:** Build Ahsora's most human, credible and distinctive brand page —
centered on the real founder journey, but making the story about *why Ahsora
exists* rather than simply a founder biography.

**Change from the v6 hybrid:** merged the two future-facing sections (Where We're
Going + The Community We Want to Build) into one, since both are forward-looking
and keeping them separate risked four consecutive "how we relate to students"
sections in a row for a page representing a currently founder-led academy. One
competitor reference ("International Council") from v6's internal strategy notes
could not be verified and has been flagged rather than repeated as fact.

**Change from v7 (this version):** Section 03 reframed as a first-person
realization rather than an abstract industry-problems list; the Philosophy
section now has a launch-safe fallback headline for use before the "help each
other" mechanism is fully operational; "Real Human Support" is defined more
scalably to avoid implying unlimited founder access; a short "why this journey
matters" bridge was folded into Section 04 rather than added as a new section;
and the Final CTA now leads with the brand rather than the emotional line.

---

## 0. Non-Negotiables

- This page must be original. Do not copy Future Meds Academy, Testbusters, IMAT
  Mentor, EnterMedSchool, or any other competitor's headline, section order,
  wording, timeline, or visual treatment.
- The founder story must be based on the founder's real experience. Do not invent
  hardship, events, achievements, student counts, or emotional details.
- Founder credentials must remain accurate and current.
- Never imply that the founder's university status guarantees student admission.
- Do not exaggerate current academy size, team size, or student outcomes.
- No invented testimonials, statistics, student results, or university destinations.
- No admission, scholarship, or visa guarantees.
- Real founder/team/product imagery only where actual assets exist. Do not
  present AI-generated people or fake product UI as real.
- The About page is the canonical long-form founder/mission story. Other pages
  use short references and link here rather than repeating the full story.

## 1. Strategic Positioning

The page communicates, in order:
```
FIRSTHAND EXPERIENCE → REALIZATION OF A PROBLEM → AHSORA'S PURPOSE →
AHSORA'S BELIEFS → HOW AHSORA OPERATES → THE PEOPLE → THE FUTURE
```

Do not make the page feel like a founder biography. The founder is the human
entry point; the mission is the subject.

**Core brand idea:**
*"We built Ahsora from firsthand experience of the journey to Medicine in Italy —
so the next student can navigate it with more clarity, structure and human
guidance."*

## 6. Section 03 — The Turning Point

```
H2: The Problem Wasn't Always the Student.

Through my own preparation, I kept noticing the same three things:

TOO MUCH INFORMATION — students can find information but struggle to identify
what matters.

TOO LITTLE GUIDANCE — a syllabus tells students what exists, not always how to
approach it.

TOO MANY DECISIONS — the IMAT is only one part of university choice,
applications and the wider journey.

Closing line: "I believed there had to be a better way."
```

**Why frame it as a personal realization, not an industry analysis:** the three
problems can be written honestly right now, since they're a real pattern you can
attest to noticing — the fix isn't removing the structure, it's keeping it
anchored in first person so this reads as one continuous story with Section 02,
not a separate "market problems" slide inserted into the middle of it.

**Design:** a visual narrative, not a feature-card wall.

## 10. Section 07 — The Ahsora Method

```
H2: We Turn Preparation Into a Process.
LEARN → PRACTICE → TEST → ANALYZE → IMPROVE → REPEAT

Learn — understand the concept.
Practice — apply it through relevant questions.
Test — work under realistic exam conditions.
Analyze — identify errors, timing problems and weak areas.
Improve — target the gaps.
Repeat — return to the cycle stronger.

Closing line: "Not more studying. Better-directed studying."
```

Keep this concise — detailed course methodology belongs on `/courses` and `/imat`.

none of the competitors reviewed use. Keep it short and direct — its power is in the directness.

## 18. Motion & Visual Language

This page should feel calmer and more narrative than `/courses`.

**Use:**
- Restrained fade-up reveals
- One subtle parallax moment in the founder story (Section 02) — not repeated elsewhere
- A gentle Learning Cycle reveal (Section 07)
- Real-image transitions
- Generous whitespace where emotional pacing benefits from it

**Avoid:**
- Constant floating objects
- Excessive card animations
- Rotating medical icons
- Confetti
- Aggressive scroll effects
- Continuous motion throughout the page

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and provide static fallbacks throughout.

---

## 19. Horizontal Space & Layout

```
WIDE sections: Hero · Founder story · Ahsora Today · People/Faculty ·
Technology showcase · any real-image storytelling section

NARROW reading sections: Founder narrative text · Philosophy statement ·
Values prose · FAQ
```

Do not place wide images/cards inside a narrow text container. Use responsive
viewport-based side padding rather than a fixed narrow wrapper. The page should
feel editorial: wide visual moments plus focused reading moments, not one
container width for everything.
