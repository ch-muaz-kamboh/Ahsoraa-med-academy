# Ahsoraa Med Academy — university directory factual audit
Audit completed: 10 October 2026 (UTC); website inspection and source checks conducted 9–10 October.  
Website: https://ahsoraa-med-academy.vercel.app/universities  
Admission year: **2026/27**. Historical examination year: **2025**, separately from 2026 places.

**Publication verdict: substantial corrections required.** The current directory should not be advertised as fully fact-checked. The findings below distinguish confirmed discrepancies from unsupported claims. “Unverified” means that the claim should not be published as established fact; it does not necessarily mean that the opposite is true.

## Scope and limits
Inspected the directory and all 26 linked profiles, including their overview, admission, hospitals, costs/scholarships and curriculum sections, plus the eight historical cutoff views (2022–2025, EU and non-EU). Compared material claims with ministry allocations, university calls, fee regulations and programme pages.

This is a correction report, not a certification of every sentence. In particular, a complete official series of final EU cutoff scores, every historical non-EU closure, every hospital affiliation and every public-university fee maximum was not established. Those items are explicitly marked for removal, qualification or further documentary verification. No website content was changed.

## 4. Cutoffs — the present historical dataset is not publication-ready

**Hide the existing 2022–2025 cutoff tables and undated profile cutoff badges until each record has a documentary source.** Several rows are demonstrably impossible; the remaining rows are not thereby proved false, but cannot inherit a “verified” label.

Problems:
- Florence and Padua MedTech have pre-launch scores.
- Siena's dentistry scores are represented as medicine.
- Catania has a 2025 EU cutoff even though its 2025 English-course EU allocation was zero.
- Profile cutoff numbers duplicate the site's 2024 final column without saying “2024.”
- The same seat allocations are reused across historical years. Historical scores must use that year's programme and quota.
- “First round,” “final,” “last offered” and “last enrolled” are treated as interchangeable.
- “Seats left after round 1” and “still assigning” have no dated evidence. Unknown data must not be represented as an ongoing admission process.
- “Source: Universitaly” without a dated ranking/round is insufficient to reproduce a university-specific final cutoff.
